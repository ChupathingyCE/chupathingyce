permissions are kept on file and can be shown on request.

## Used under their own licenses

These need no special permission; we follow their licenses and credit the
authors.

- **OpenCE, before its license change.** Code merged into OpenCE before
  2026-10-19 07:12 UTC, when it moved to the AGPL, was released under terms
  that let us use it. We take nothing added after that time.
- **OpenH1, before its license change.** Commits authored before the same
  time. Later work by the OpenH1 team's own authors is covered by their
  permission above; OpenCE's later code carried in OpenH1 is not, and we
  don't take it.
- **mastercheese-dev/OpenCE.** Commits by that fork's own authors before
  2026-10-28 21:18 UTC, while the fork was CC0.
- **Chimera** (SnowyMouse and contributors) and **Demon** and **Invader**
  (Aerocatia), all GPL-3.0: fixes ported from them carry credit.
- **reclaimer** (Sigmmma), GPL-3.0: the Stubbs the Zombie map layouts
  Stubbs mode reads.
- **Third-party libraries, fonts and art** in `port/third_party`,
  `port/assets` and `port/linux/ui/fonts`: their licenses sit beside them.

## Credit

Every change taken from someone else's work says so in its commit message,
by the author's handle, and keeps their authorship or a co-author line where
the change is substantially theirs. Release notes thank them too.

Questions about any of this: open an issue, or ask in the Chupathingy
Discord.
