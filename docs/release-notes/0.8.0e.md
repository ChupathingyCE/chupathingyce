- **Surround sound.** 5.1 and 7.1 output, as with Dolby Digital on the Xbox, picked automatically from your speakers or set in the new **Audio Setup** screen. The same screen has Headphone 3D (off by default) and Doppler, so Warthogs and Banshees change pitch as they pass and sounds are shaped by their cones.
- **Movies.** The intro, attract and cutscene movies play on Windows, Mac, Linux and Android. The disc import now copies the disc's `bink` folder; a movie that is missing is skipped.
- **Xbox map packs.** Community packs of Xbox maps live in `maps_<pack>` folders beside `maps`, Halo CE+ in `maps_cex` (`maps_ceplus` works too). A pack is recognized by its maps, not by its folder's name.
- **Grifball and Bomb** are in, with a court to play them on.
- **Online co-op.** Multiplayer > CREATE GAME > INTERNET, then switch the map list's top row to SINGLEPLAYER, pick a level and a difficulty, and go on to Server Setup.
- **Controllers.** Gamepad buttons can be rebound, and the pause menu's background is fixed.
- **Invite links** are copied to the clipboard only if you turn on `network.copy_to_clipboard` (off by default).
- **Chupathingy menus:** attract mode works, the sniper scope has no gap, and the loading screen's time can be set. The console's own text is drawn only while the console is open.
- Small parity fixes between the platforms, and the shared code for the Wii U, Xbox 360 and Switch ports, with the byte order of console networking fixed so consoles and PCs play together.
