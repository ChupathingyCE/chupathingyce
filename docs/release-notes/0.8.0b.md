Hotfix 2 of 2 for 0.8.0.

- **The high-res HUD, from Aerocatia's and Jessie's halopc-restored art.** The HUD on the Xbox maps is now their art (release r220) almost everywhere: reticles, meters, scopes, the sniper ladder, weapon pickups, button prompts, the Reload, No Ammo and No Grenades warnings, and the vehicle seat plates. The new rocket launcher reticle and the gaps listed in #126 are filled. Reticle spacing is the original's, so grenade tricks still line up, and the sniper zoom still says 10x. Other languages' discs keep their own text. Thank you to Aerocatia and Jessie.
- **Campaign:** no melee with no weapon in hand, as on the Xbox and PC.
- **Big Custom Edition maps:** maps over 200 MB (Bigass V3 and others) are checked and joined like any other; the limit is now 1 GB.
- **Android:** large Custom Edition maps that ran out of tag cache (Precipice and others) load; and on handhelds where the game couldn't reserve its memory (AYN, Retroid), it now says so and offers to switch to the 64-bit build, keeping your data and saves.
- Online Games: a held trigger changes the sort once, not every frame (it raced through the sorts), and a held button on Online Games or the Moderation screen acts once a press. A held d-pad steps at the menus' pace, as it does since 0.8.0 in the menus, Online Games and the Moderation screen.
- Carnage report: the host's Quit and Pick Game prompt stays inside the 4:3 screen; with the keyboard's keys it ran past the right edge.
- Android: the back button and the back gesture back out of the menus again, and the Chupathingy menus' prompts (Defaults, Cancel, Accept and the rest) answer a tap, as they answer a mouse click.
- Volume: a change to audio.volume, audio.music_volume or audio.effects_volume saved in config.toml while the game runs now applies within a second; before, it waited for the next start.
