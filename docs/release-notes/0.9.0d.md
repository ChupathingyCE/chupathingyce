- **Security: Custom Edition sounds.** A Custom Edition map with a broken sound could make the game read memory past the end of a table while playing it. Fixed. If you download Custom Edition maps, please update.
- **Online Games keeps live games.** When a lobby server sent its whole list at once, old listings could push live games out, so they went missing from Online Games. They stay now.
- **No more holes in the maps.** Cliffs and walls near portal windows (Blood Gulch near blue base, Timberline, Coldsnap) flickered or went missing. Fixed.
- **Smoother at high frame rates.** Nav points, lights on objects, contrails, change colors and camo, particle streams from vehicles and the camera shake all move smoothly between ticks now. Wind steps once a tick instead of with your frame rate.
- **Sharper picture.** Mirrors and reflections are drawn at your resolution instead of the Xbox's 320x240. Decals don't flicker on walls at high resolutions. The scope zoom costs less at high resolutions.
- **Field of view fixes.** The sniper scope's marks stay together at a wide field of view. The plasma pistol's and plasma rifle's glows stay on the gun when the weapon has its own field of view. Screen fog stays on the vehicle canopy.
- **Weapons on Custom Edition maps.** The flamethrower's stream and sound, and the fuel rod, fire the way they do in Halo PC.
- **Little things you'd notice.** With Full model detail on, the Chief keeps his head in the cryo tube. The loading screen keeps its 4:3 shape on wide windows. High-res text fits a Custom Edition loc.map's narrower letters, and the high-res HUD and text switch right away on a still menu.
- **Menus and controls.** Keyboard prompts show the keys that work (the Pillar of Autumn look prompt, the sniper tutorial, quitting the carnage report). Settings values stay white after the mouse passes over them. The profile screens' info box shimmers with its list again. Name entry fills a wide screen. Fast setup opens the same lobby the menus do. Unplugging a controller no longer leaks it.
- **Windows.** Borderless is an ordinary window, not a topmost popup, so switching away no longer goes black for a few seconds on some displays. OBS game capture shows the picture the right way up. A map file is closed when the game is done with it.
- **Linux and Android.** On Linux the mouse is held and centered even without XInput2, and a read-only game folder keeps what it has. On Android a captured mouse aims and clicks, and the touch controls redraw less.
- **A little faster.** Sound mixing takes about a third less time, with the same sound. The game reads the clock only when something needs it.
