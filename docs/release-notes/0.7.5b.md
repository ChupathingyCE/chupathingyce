## What's new in 0.7.5b

**The campaign starts at the beginning again.** New profiles start at The Pillar of Autumn and open each level as you finish the one before it, as on the Xbox. Earlier versions gave every new profile the whole campaign. Profiles you already have keep what they had. To start one over, open the console and type `profile_reset_campaign`; it tells you what it will do, and `profile_reset_campaign yes` does it. If you liked everything open, set `game.unlock_all_levels = true` in config.toml.

**Checkpoints work at high frame rates.** The game measured its wait for a safe checkpoint in frames instead of game time, so above 30 frames a second most checkpoints gave up. Campaign checkpoints now come as often as they did on the Xbox.

**Co-op**
- When every player dies a second time, the game respawns you at the checkpoint instead of waiting forever.
- Enemy grenades are visible to the players who joined the host.

**Menus**
- The PC menus list Custom Edition and HaloMD maps when you host a game, after the Xbox maps.
- The campaign pause menu has Settings (PC menus), in the Xbox's own box.
- The main menu's background and music keep playing in Settings and the multiplayer screens.
- Changing `display.high_res_text` while playing takes effect right away.
- Change Name during a game no longer crashes.
- The old Xbox build number on the main menu is gone. The game's version is shown faintly in its place, and in a game too if you turn on `display.version_watermark`.

**New settings**
- `display.field_of_view` (Mouse Setup in the PC menus): 70 is the Xbox's view; up to 120.
- `display.match_timer`: the time left in a timed multiplayer game, at the top of the screen. Off by default.
- `display.server_messages`: turn server warnings and notices off, or pick their colors with `display.server_message_text_color`, `_border_color` and `_background_color`.

**Fixes**
- A draining overshield no longer sounds like you're being shot.
- Busy fights on PC no longer drop explosions, particles and lights.
- Android: **Import maps** adds Custom Edition maps with your phone's own file picker, no file manager needed. Long-press the app icon, or find it in the touch controls' Options.
- Linux: the mouse wheel works under X11, and building from a folder whose path has a space says so clearly.

Thanks to everyone who reported these in the Discord.
