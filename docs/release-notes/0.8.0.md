## What's new in 0.8.0 (Unreleased)

**License.** From this release, Chupathingy is free software under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later. Releases up to and including 0.7.5d stay CC0, as they were released. The README's [License](https://github.com/ChupathingyCE/chupathingyce#license) section has the details.

**Text chat.** Chupathingy players can talk in games hosted by Chupathingy, as on Halo PC: T says a line to everyone, Y to your team, Enter sends and Escape cancels. Lines show at the lower left in the HUD's own font, names in their team's color, and fade after a few seconds. H shows the recent lines and the players, where M mutes someone for you alone. On a controller, D-pad up picks a quick line and D-pad down says it. Chat only passes between Chupathingy copies and servers; OpenCE players in the same game see nothing different, and nothing that is said is written down anywhere.
- Server messages arrive in the chat too: a dedicated server's `sv_say` (or Say on its control panel) shows as a "Server" line to every Chupathingy player.
- Blocked words are starred out by default (`chat.filter`: mask, hide or show), from the blocked names list and your own `chat_filter.txt`. Nobody is ever kicked for a word.
- Turn it off with `chat.enabled = false`, show only your team with `chat.show = "team"`, or hide names with `chat.streamer_mode`. The keys are in Controls Setup.
- Hosting: `network.chat` (on, team or off) and `network.chat_filter`.

**Follow camera.** When the wait to respawn is long (a game type with a long respawn time, out of lives, or odd man out), your camera follows a living teammate from behind once the death camera is done, as in Halo 2 and 3; in a game without teams it follows your killer or the leader. LB and RB (Q and E, or the mouse wheel) switch who, and a small "FOLLOWING" line says who it is. A short respawn keeps the original death camera. It is your view only, so it works in any game, and each split screen player has their own. `display.follow_camera` (long_waits, always or off) and `display.follow_camera_after` (seconds, 5 by default) choose when.

**Chupathingy menus, the new default.** The menus now come in three views, chosen by `display.menus`:
- **Chupathingy** (`"chupathingy"`, the new default): the Xbox's menus and look, with Halo PC's screens added where they help. The main menu has CAMPAIGN, MULTIPLAYER, PROFILES, SETTINGS and QUIT in the Xbox's font and glow. SETTINGS has every settings page (Controls, Gamepads, Mouse, Audio, Video, Network Setup and Privacy) with the same rows and help as Halo PC's, laid out as Xbox screens with the Xbox's B, A and Y prompts. PROFILES shows your profiles beside the Xbox's profile card. The pause menu has SETTINGS and, for the host, END GAME. MULTIPLAYER has Online Games, and the clipboard invite question uses the Xbox's question box. The gametype cards list each gametype's main rules.
- **Xbox** (`"xbox"`): as close to the console's menus as possible, with only what Chupathingy needs added (Online Games, joining, the host's END GAME, the button prompts).
- **Gearbox** (`"gearbox"`, `"pc"` still works): as close to Gearbox's Halo PC as possible, with its Server Browser.

If your `config.toml` sets `display.menus` (for example `"xbox"`, the old default, written by an earlier version), it keeps that value; remove the line or set `"chupathingy"` to try the new view.

**Dedicated server**
- The log is now `logs/debug.txt` in the data folder (`/data/logs/debug.txt` in Docker), not `/data/debug.txt`: update anything that reads the old path. No file is left at the old path. The Docker image keeps its cache in `/data/cache` (`HALO_CACHE_ROOT`), and `HALO_LOG_ROOT` moves the log.
- `sv_say`, `sv_chat [on|team|off]`, `sv_mute <player> [minutes]` and `sv_unmute`. Moderators can mute a player's chat from the in-game Moderation screen or the control panel; the muted player's game tells them.
- A welcome message: `sv_welcome <text>` (or `HALO_DEDICATED_WELCOME`) sends each Chupathingy player a server message in their chat as they join. Thanks to thelp60 and Cryosphered for the idea.
- The carnage report's length is a setting: `HALO_DEDICATED_POSTGAME_SECONDS` (`postgame_seconds`), 20 by default.
- `HALO_DEDICATED_SKIP_LOBBY = true` starts the next game right after the carnage report when enough players stayed, without waiting out the lobby's countdown. Off by default.
- Chat flooding: once a player says lines faster than chat allows, their chat pauses for 5 seconds and their game says "Chat paused for 5 seconds" (`HALO_DEDICATED_CHAT_COOLDOWN`; 0 turns the pause off). Games hosted by players pause it for 5 seconds too.

**Changes**
- In the map lists of every view, Halo PC and HaloMD maps that Halo PC's own list doesn't describe show what the map file says: its spawn points, and "Supports vehicles" when it has any. HaloMD's copies of Halo PC's maps get Halo PC's description and picture.
- Sharper text looks more like Halo: the high-res text and Online Games now use Cyborg Interface Gothic, a free font made for this by fizzy to match Halo's interface font (SIL Open Font License). Thanks, fizzy.
- Button prompts follow the keyboard: after you use the keyboard or mouse, the HUD names the bound key ("Hold [E] to pick up", "Hold Tab for score") and the Xbox menus' A/B/X/Y prompts show key caps, as Halo PC did. Pressing a controller button switches back. `input.keyboard_prompts = false` keeps the controller's buttons. Thanks to OpenRift for the idea.
- Use your own font for the sharp text and Online Games: put a `.ttf` or `.otf` in the new `data/fonts` folder beside `maps` and set `display.high_res_font = "YourFont.ttf"` (`display.high_res_font_large` sets one for the large text only). A file that can't be read is noted in the log and the built-in font is used.
- The game's logs have a folder of their own: `logs/`, beside `maps` (`logs/debug.txt`; on Windows also `halo.log`; and crash reports waiting to be sent, which on Windows were beside `halo.exe`). For a bug report, attach what is in `logs/`. Nothing is moved: an old `debug.txt` beside `maps` is left as it was, and crash reports still waiting in the old place are sent from there. New settings `paths.logs` and `paths.cache` put the logs and the cache elsewhere.
- Video Setup is shorter: Frame Rate Limit and Smooth Motion are one Frame Rate row, where 30 (Xbox) keeps the game's own frames and the others blend between them. Instant Aim moved to config.toml alone (`display.direct_camera`, still on by default). Thanks to OpenRift for the suggestion.
- A game you host starts with room for 16 players, not 128, in Server Setup; the list still goes up to 128. Thanks to OpenRift for the suggestion.
- Large maps no longer cut off in the distance: `display.far_clip_distance` (1024 as on the Xbox by default, up to 10000) sets how far the world is drawn, and it holds across map loads and joins. It changes the picture only. Thanks to Ghost901 for the idea.
- Audio Setup can choose the output device the sound plays on (Output Device), or leave it on Default to follow the system's. It switches as soon as you press OK. In config.toml: `audio.device`. Thanks to MobSlicer152 for the idea.
- Chupathingy keeps its own saves on Windows and Linux; your profiles and game types are copied over the first time.
- Online Games looks like the Xbox's menus: their fonts, glow and panels, larger rows, the Xbox's buttons for a controller (key caps for the keyboard), and a tag on every game's map for its family (XBX, HCE, HMD, GBX). A game with a password shows a lock, and the ping column shows the round trip to a host you are connected to.
- Online Games shows each game's ping before you join. Hosts on this version answer a quick ping (it never takes a slot); for older hosts you get an estimate from the game's region, shown as `~85`. Sorting by Ping lists measured pings first, then estimates. No pings with online play off, and `network.ping_games = false` turns direct pings off.
- A Privacy screen in Settings (Chupathingy and Gearbox menus, after Network Setup) shows what this copy sends the game list, each with a line on what it means: the stats of the games you host and join, Delta Stats, and crash reports.
- New `network.report_hosted_games`: set it to false (or turn off Hosted Game Stats) to keep your hosted games listed while sending no carnage reports or stats of them. Before, the only way was to stop listing them. On by default, as before. Dedicated servers: `HALO_NET_REPORT_HOSTED=false`.
- The game asks before joining an invite link it finds on the clipboard when you switch to it: "Join the game from your clipboard?", with the game's name and map when the game list knows them, and Yes or No (A or B, Enter or Escape, or a click). No is remembered for that invite. In a game, a line at the top says an invite is waiting, and the question shows when you pause. `network.join_from_clipboard` is now `"ask"` (the default), `"always"` (join without asking, as before) or `"never"`; an older `true` or `false` still works, as always or never. Network Setup in the Chupathingy and Gearbox menus has the same three choices.
- The host of a multiplayer game gets END GAME in the pause menu with the Xbox menus too (RESUME GAME, END GAME, LEAVE GAME), as Halo PC and the Gearbox menus have it: the game ends as its time limit would and the players stay for the next. Other players' pause menus are unchanged.
- Discord now shows Chupathingy for players whose settings still named the old application.
- Powerup timers: `display.powerup_timers = true` shows the seconds left on your active camouflage, overshield and full spectrum vision in a small HUD line, for each split screen player. Off by default. Thanks to Chloe Price for the idea.
- The classic loading screen: `display.loading_screen_min_seconds` keeps the original loading screen up for at least that many seconds when a level loads, as on the Xbox, where loads are now usually too quick to see it. 0 (the default) is no wait. It applies to campaign and split screen only; games on the network load as fast as they can. Thanks to OpenRift and Vuthakral for the idea.
- The disc extraction at the first start also copies the Xbox disc's Bink movies (the intro, attract and credits movies) into `bink/` beside `maps`, on every platform, and only the files that are missing. Thanks to OpenRift for the idea.

**Fixes**
- When a Custom Edition map is too big for the tag cache, debug.txt now says plainly how much room it needs and how much there is.
- A Custom Edition map the game can't play now says why on screen (for example, that Custom Edition's bitmaps.map is missing from maps_ce, or that the map is too big for the tag cache), in place of the damaged disc message.
- Android: Link Profile works again after the first run, and your finished games are confirmed on the game list. The game no longer refused its own player key.
- Android: the menus' Quit closes the game; it did nothing before.
- Android: the status and navigation bars stay hidden in the game, also after the Options screen or a dialog; a swipe from the edge shows them for a moment. Thanks to Sabriel for the report.
- Android: on Snapdragon (Adreno) graphics the game unpacks the maps' compressed textures itself, which should end the speckled ground and walls Sabriel reported. `display.texture_decode = "gpu"` goes back to the driver's.
- The in-game Moderation screen steps a row at a time at the menus' pace when a direction is held (a frame at a time before, too fast to stop on a player). Thanks to S-73 G. for reporting the menus scrolling too fast.
- The game window is always opaque: on some Windows drivers (and some Linux desktops) the desktop showed through the menus. Thanks to pugzysixtyfour for the report.
- Co-op: a crash from a player who had left (a stale player handle) is fixed. Clients no longer fall through elevators going up, a scripted teleport brings the whole co-op team inside, the scripts' look checks work for every player, and a respawned vehicle keeps the name its teleporter uses.
- Joining your first game after searching no longer hangs.
- Grenade blasts now count their hits; before, a few of them were refused by the host. A client's copy of another player no longer throws phantom grenades as it spawns, and a player who goes through a teleporter stays where they land instead of bouncing back.
- Flashlights and vehicle lights now stay right in games with OpenCE players (network version 25's light state).
- Bodies no longer float or sit in walls on clients in big games.
- The lobby countdown no longer starts over every time someone joins or leaves.
- A server message ending in `|` no longer makes the HUD read past its end.
- Maps from anywhere are checked harder: a tag name hidden in tag data is dropped, a map whose globals lack the camera or player information is refused, and long tag names can no longer overflow the debug text.
- Android: string data in the game code is no longer corrupted by the build (a leading `_` was dropped).
- Smaller fixes: leaving the postgame host screen goes back to the main menu; Create Game options open without a one-second pause; a saved profile's controls apply right away; any controller drives the menus when one person plays; vehicle respawns never take another spawn's vehicle or delete one someone is riding; items rest on surfaces of large Custom Edition maps; fewer stalls and smoother camera after a long frame; less CPU use in the 30 fps limiter.
- The original loading screen draws again: a load slow enough to show it (a first load from a slow disk) shows the picture and its glow, where the screen was black.

Thanks to the people who wrote these fixes in the Halo CE decomp community: Paul Hontz, xshxdex98 (Shade), hkmodd, eltanschauung, justinfilip, Luke, MrBruh, Addison Bair, thelinkin3000, Brendan Turner and chestahh.
