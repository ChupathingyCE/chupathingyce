**Chupathingy 0.9.2a: SPV3 and OpenSauce are in open testing, plus fixes.**

### SPV3 and OpenSauce

Both are early, and only on the 64-bit Windows, Mac and Linux builds.

- **Install SPV3 from the setup screen.** Go to MAPS and pick ADD SPV3. Point it at SPV3 3.3.0's release zip or the folder it unpacked to. Bring your own copy. If it stops partway, run it again and it carries on.
- **SPV3 plays from its own menus.** Pick a level and a difficulty and it starts. A new profile's first campaign starts too.
- **SPV3's own scripts run out of the box.** No setting needed.
- **Post-processing** on OpenGL and bgfx.
- **Skies and lighting** change when SPV3's scripts ask.
- **Movies** play at start and in the credits.
- **All four grenade types,** cluster and anti-matter too.
- **OpenSauce maps outside SPV3.** Put them in `maps_os`. A mod with its own menus goes in its own `maps_<name>` folder and shows up in Play.
- Maps that need OpenSauce's extras play offline and on System Link for now.

Thanks to the SPV3 team for making SPV3.

### Fixes

- Workshop pages show an item's whole description.
- DIRECT IP is a row in System Link's list now, not only the X button.
- Make the folders also makes `maps_cex`, `maps_spv3` and `maps_mcc`.
- Video Setup's labels and help text are back on their own rows.
- The vehicle seat view no longer judders at 144 Hz or uncapped. The third-person camera doesn't pop.
- Big Custom Edition and OpenSauce mods no longer lose sounds when the decoded sound file fills up.
- Closing the game during a hung load no longer sends the next start back to OpenGL from Vulkan.
- Nightlies stop asking to update to the release they already have.
- An unknown command in init.txt no longer crashes the game at start.
- A script that uses an object after it's gone no longer crashes the game. By BirchWood.
- Custom HUDs on Custom Edition maps like Shipment no longer draw black.
- Custom Edition map pictures show in Select Map with MCC's or Halo PC's bitmaps.map. By BirchWood.
- MCC maps: long objectives wrap, the settings pictures are right, and the vehicle health panel and counter outlines are MCC's own. Mostly by BirchWood.
- The sniper scope's ticks and the HD HUD's counters sit right. By BirchWood.
- Stubbs: armed humans carry their weapons again. His hit reactions, animation effects and extra surface types are back.
- CE+ Xbox's Museum no longer draws its frame twice.
- Lens flares clear on a BSP switch, and muzzle flashes and rockets light what's around them. From chestahh's GulchCE work.
- On a 32-bit Windows PC, the game says where to get Chupathingy32.

### Under the hood

- CI runs in tiers with a merge queue. Code nothing used is gone.
- FFmpeg's license is in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt now.

### Known issues

- SPV3 and OpenSauce are early. Expect rough edges.
- In online co-op, SPV3's extra grenades and its sky and lighting changes don't reach the players who join.
- On bgfx with Vulkan or Direct3D 11 and anti-aliasing on, SPV3's depth effects don't draw. OpenGL and Metal draw them.
- A few Custom Edition maps' own pause menus show up blank. Press Start or Esc again to close it ([#1866](https://github.com/ChupathingyCE/chupathingyce/issues/1866)).
- Online co-op: the map list shows multiplayer maps too. Riders can fall through moving elevators, and cutscene cameras and BSP switches can go wrong.
- Joining as the game starts sometimes times out.
- A crash can happen some time after a split screen player leaves a game.
- Android can lag and run hot.
- The Frame Rate row does nothing with V-Sync on.
- Clips play without video on Discord and in browsers.
- A Workshop download offered at a map change ends in an error.

Found a bug? Post it in #bug-reports on our Discord (or #reportes in Spanish), with your debug.txt from [dump.chupathingy.org](https://dump.chupathingy.org). For SPV3, put "SPV3" in the title and say which level you were on.
