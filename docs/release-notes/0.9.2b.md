**Chupathingy 0.9.2b: your settings back, and a question for Mac players on Metal.**

### Fixes

- **Settings kept on update.** 0.9.2a's Windows and Linux 64-bit downloads had a blank config.toml in them. Updating put it over your settings and kept yours as config.toml.old. 0.9.2b puts your old settings back on its own, and updates never touch config.toml again.
- **Mac:** if you picked Metal in 0.9.1, the game asks once whether to go back to OpenGL. Metal is still in testing. It has a bug where some models, the HUD and overlays go missing.

Everything else is the same as 0.9.2a. Its notes are on the [0.9.2a release](https://github.com/ChupathingyCE/chupathingyce/releases/tag/v0.9.2a).

Found a bug? Post it in #bug-reports on our Discord (or #reportes in Spanish), with your debug.txt from [dump.chupathingy.org](https://dump.chupathingy.org).
