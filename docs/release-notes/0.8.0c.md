- **Linux and Steam Deck: Link Profile works again, and your stats stay yours.** Since Chupathingy moved to its own save folder, the copy of your player key there could be read by other users, so the game refused it ("no player key") and Link Profile failed. The game now makes your key private again and keeps it; nothing to do on your side. Don't delete game_list_player.key: it's your identity, and a new one starts a new service record.
- If you already deleted it: your original is still in `~/.local/share/halo-linux/game_list_player.key`. Copy it back into `~/.local/share/chupathingyce/` and start the game.
