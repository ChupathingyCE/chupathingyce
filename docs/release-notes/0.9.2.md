**Chupathingy 0.9.2: a real game lobby, online co-op, Direct IP, Custom Edition players in every stock game type, Forge, sound closer to the Xbox, and early SPV3 support. Please update.**

Chupathingy no longer removes a halo.exe that isn't its own. Since 0.9.0, a Halo PC halo.exe in the same folder as Chupathingy could be deleted at start. That's fixed here.

### What's new

- **A game lobby.** A Halo 2 style lobby for every way into a game in the Chupathingy menus: players, teams, ready checks, the map card and the host's menu.
- **Online co-op** from the co-op menu. A level you win together unlocks the next one on every machine.
- **Direct IP is back.** Join a game by its address or host name. Your recent addresses are kept.
- **Custom Edition players.** Custom Edition 1.0.10 players on our servers can switch weapons, zoom, and play Team Slayer, Capture the Flag, Oddball, King of the Hill and Race. Up to 16 of them in a game.
- **SWAT and Hardcore** play and host.
- **Content Evolved.** Its plain games (Slayer, CTF and the other stock games) can be joined. Its modded games say so before you join.
- **Forge**, by BirchWood. A layout editor for the multiplayer maps and campaign missions, with AI characters, campaign vehicles, a flyable Pelican and dropship, and kill, damage and gravity zones. Opt-in, local games only.
- **Ragdolls** for dead bodies, by BirchWood. Opt-in.
- **HDR.** Tone mapping on any screen, and real HDR output on an HDR display. Opt-in.
- **Developer flames.** Chupathingy's developers get a blue flame on their helmet, like Bungie's in Halo 2. It shows only for a verified developer whose identity your own game has checked. `display.developer_flames` turns it off.
- **Sharper text** everywhere, crisp at any resolution.
- **Sound closer to the Xbox:** its resampler, its loudness, its reverb and obstruction, and quiet sounds fade out as they did.
- **A drop-down console** with a filter, colors and history that's kept.
- **A [tuning] section** in config.toml with presets (retail, lan-party, potato) and performance knobs.
- **bgfx** gets FXAA and SMAA, anisotropic filtering and supersampling. Picking Vulkan on the setup screen sticks now.
- **Early SPV3 support:** OpenSauce maps, their scripts, their post-processing and Chimera's Lua API. Experimental, desktop only, bring your own copy.

### Little things

- Clips are in Settings now, with their length. No config.toml needed.
- Gyro aiming on PC, with any controller that has a gyro. Opt-in.
- Button prompts for PlayStation and Switch controllers.
- Vehicle field of view, zoom sensitivity and a lowered crosshair option.
- HUD safe zones, and split screen views centered.

### Important changes

- **Sound is 6 dB quieter by default.** That's the original Xbox's level. If you liked it louder, set Loudness to Louder in Audio Setup (`audio.loudness = "louder"`). An older config.toml is asked once.
- **Dedicated servers take 16 players by default** instead of 12. Set your own number with `HALO_DEDICATED_MAXIMUM_PLAYERS`, the settings file or `sv_maxplayers`.
- **halo.exe is gone.** Chupathingy starts as chupathingy.exe (chupathingy on Linux and FreeBSD). Update any shortcut, script or Steam entry that still starts halo.exe or halo.
- **Updating from 0.7.5d or older:** the old updater can't restart the game. Start chupathingy yourself once, and it cleans up the old copy.
- The drop-down console, the game lobby, the join screen and the sharper text are on by default. Each one has a setting to go back.

### Security

- Hosts check the game's password on every kind of join.
- Hosts answer game searches from the internet only when Direct IP hosting is on, and at a capped rate.
- More checks on Custom Edition maps, the map loader and Workshop list names.

### Known issues

- A few Custom Edition maps have their own pause menu, and on some it shows up blank. Press Start or Esc again to close it. Settings are in the main menu and the Delta Guide. Proper support is coming in 0.9.3 ([#1866](https://github.com/ChupathingyCE/chupathingyce/issues/1866)).
- SPV3 is early. Its post-processing draws only with OpenGL, and b30's intro is too bright. A blue square sits at the crosshair. Some of its AI, its four grenade types and parts of its menus aren't there yet. Lua and SPV3 mode are desktop only.
- Forge is local only. Playing a host's layout online comes after New Year.
- Online co-op: the map list shows multiplayer maps too. Riders can fall through moving elevators, and cutscene cameras and BSP switches can go wrong.
- Joining as the game starts sometimes times out.
- A crash can happen some time after a split screen player leaves a game.
- Android can lag and run hot.
- Video Setup's Interface labels are one row off.
- The Frame Rate row does nothing with V-Sync on.
- Clips play without video on Discord and in browsers.
- A Workshop download offered at a map change ends in an error.
- The vehicle and third-person camera can pop at 144 Hz and uncapped.

Found something else? Post it in #bug-reports on our Discord (or #reportes in Spanish), with your debug.txt from [dump.chupathingy.org](https://dump.chupathingy.org).
