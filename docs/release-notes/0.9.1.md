**Chupathingy 0.9.1: the Delta Guide with friends, parties, clans and voice, Halo MCC Workshop maps in beta, the Xbox's own 3D sound, cross-play with OpenCE 44, and an early look at Stubbs the Zombie.**

### What's new

- **The Delta Guide.** One panel over whatever is on screen, the menus or a game in progress. Press F3, your controller's Guide button, or pick it in the pause menu; B puts you back where you were. It has Players here, Friends, Party, Clan, Service Record and Settings, in the Chupathingy and Xbox menus alike. Five looks: Halo Blue, Dashboard Green (with the Xbox dashboard's cellwall behind it), Visor Orange, Live Orange and Chupathingy Red.
- **Friends.** Link your Delta profile and your friends show up with what they're playing. Join them, invite them, and answer requests without leaving the game. Invites ring right away. Add someone from Players here or Recent.
- **Parties.** Start one and share its code, link or QR code. When the leader joins a game, the party follows, and games you host keep a party on one team when the teams stay even. Party and game links open from Discord.
- **Clans.** Your clan's members and where they're playing, clan invites, and your tag next to your name.
- **Voice chat.** Off until you turn it on in the Guide's Settings, then hold V or the D-pad right. Party, team, players near you, or everyone if the host allows it. The host decides which channels are on, and a host's mute covers voice too.
- **Verified names.** Sign in with Delta and you show by your handle, with your clan tag. Nobody else can wear it. Signing in is a short code now, and the Guide lists your devices so you can sign one out.
- **The scoreboard.** Verified players get a small Delta mark in the scoreboard's logo column, so names keep their full width. SCOREBOARD ICONS (Settings > Interface, the setup screen and the Guide) turns the logos and the mark off for a clean scoreboard of names and scores. Thanks OpenRift412 for the idea and where to put it.
- **Medals and Service Record.** Halo 3's multikills and sprees join Halo CE's. Medals on the HUD are off unless you turn them on. Service Record keeps each profile's games, kills, maps and weapons.
- **Screenshots and clips.** F9 or Print Screen saves a screenshot. Turn clips on and F10 saves the last 30 seconds.
- **Send my logs.** The Guide's Settings can send your logs mid-game and gives you a code to paste in #bug-reports. It tells you first what it takes out: your computer's account name and network addresses.
- **Update channels.** Release, Testing or Nightly, in Settings and on the setup screen. The Steam Deck updates in Game Mode, and an update you already downloaded isn't downloaded again.
- **The Xbox's own 3D sound and reverb.** Sounds around you and the echo of each room now follow models measured on a real Xbox. Same settings as before (`audio.hrtf`, `audio.reverb`). Thanks chester, who brought these models into GulchCE, and goatbutt and the OpenH1 team, who measured them.
- **Light effects.** Plasma, needles, rockets and muzzle flashes light up the level, and walls block it. On where your graphics card can run it (Video > Advanced). Closed doors block light too. Thanks chester, for writing it in GulchCE and letting us use it.
- **Missing maps.** A Custom Edition map you don't have comes from HaloNet, then maps.chupathingy.org, and every download is checked before it's used. Getting a map from the host is there too, off unless you turn it on, and it now works with OpenCE players on their current builds as well. Downloads onto another drive work now (thanks ugoboom).
- **Online Games.** Four lists (LB and RB), each game's game type, and a JOIN GAME panel that asks before you join. Bans follow the Delta account, and you can report a player on our servers from Players here.
- **Fly the debug camera with a mouse and keyboard.** Hold the middle mouse button on the flying camera: keys move, the mouse looks, R and F go up and down, the wheel sets the speed. Following Guven's and hispanorum's debug camera work.
- **Titles.** Classic titles by default, Cyborg for small text, and a Title Font setting. Pick HALO and the game rebuilds Halo's own title lettering from your own ui.map, with nothing shipped (thanks OpenRift412 for the push and the reference renders).
- **Menus.** The Gearbox menus aren't a menu set anymore: it's the Chupathingy menus and the Xbox menus. If yours was Gearbox, you get the Xbox menus and a note once. Controller prompts use Kenney's 1.5a set, with Steam Deck and Wii U buttons. Long map and level names on the map cards end with "..." instead of being cut off mid-word.
- **Licenses.** Every download has two files beside the game: LICENSE.txt and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt, with every library we use. The old loose license files are cleaned up once.

### Halo MCC Workshop maps (beta)

MCC support is beta. It works, we've played it a lot in the test builds, and it will still have rough edges. Please report what breaks.

- **Get and play Workshop maps in the game.** Own Halo: MCC on Steam, link Steam once in the Guide (scan a code), and the Workshop tab lists, searches and gets Halo 1 maps through our own downloader. PLAY takes you to the normal Host or campaign screens. Items that bring both a campaign and multiplayer maps say "Campaign + Multiplayer".
- **Already have them?** Steam's own MCC folders are found, and a Workshop folder copied whole into `maps_mcc` loads as it is, with no Steam needed (chester's work from GulchCE, thanks!).
- **Multiplayer.** Host and join MCC maps from the menus, in split screen, System Link and online, and on dedicated servers. OpenCE players can join our MCC games, and we can join theirs. If you're missing the map, the game offers it from the Workshop.
- **Campaigns.** MCC campaign scripts run on 64-bit builds, so Crash Site and most of COMBAT REVOLVED play. Checkpoints save again on MCC maps, skulls included, so a revert or Save and Quit keeps your place instead of starting the level over. MCC maps get MCC's skulls; `game.mcc_skulls` stands in for a skull menu for now (thanks ugoboom for the report and Photolysis for documenting them).
- MCC maps are never shared from a host. You get them from the Workshop yourself.

### Stubbs the Zombie: an early look

Stubbs mode is an early work in progress, off by default, and nothing in Halo changes with it off. Bring your own Stubbs the Zombie Xbox maps in a `maps_stubbs` folder and pick "Stubbs the Zombie (early)" on the setup screen.

- What works: Stubbs's menus, ONE PLAYER from the plaza, each finished level loading the next, and Save and Quit picking up where you left off, with Stubbs's saves in their own folder. TWO PLAYER split screen works from Stubbs's own menu.
- Stubbs plays from behind, sprints, punches, throws his hand and drives it, takes over humans with it, and eats brains to raise a horde.
- The image effects, the lighting on the lawn and the corn draw on OpenGL and bgfx.
- A few levels stop partway for now. On the plaza, the tutorial stops at the whistle lesson. At the police station, the hand can't climb up into the air duct yet. The boss at the mall can't be beaten yet. We reached the end of most other levels in testing, some of them with the console's help.
- Still missing: Stubbs's own HUD, many of his animations, whistle and shove, online co-op, and the horde in a saved game. Desktop and Android only; consoles leave it out.

### Fixes

- **Graphics.** Lens flares stay put (AMD included), lights that aren't a number no longer draw black patches, camo and mist draw in the right order, no dark rims around HUD meters, and the sea fogs at the horizon on The Silent Cartographer. Cutscene cameras and fast vehicle turns are smooth at high frame rates, other players' flashlights stop jumping, and screen shake hits as hard as at 30.
- **bgfx (still opt-in).** Fixes for AMD and Mesa on Vulkan, Direct3D 11 on Windows when Vulkan won't start, and drivers known to run badly are skipped. If Vulkan never gets going, the next start uses OpenGL and asks once whether to keep it. MCC maps draw their textures, and water and anisotropic filtering match OpenGL.
- **Older computers.** OpenGL 3.3 is enough now, so older integrated graphics run. Maps make their shaders on the loading screen, for fewer hitches. Fullscreen has a refresh rate setting. Windows 11 no longer slows the game down behind other windows.
- **Co-op.** Nobody stays stuck in the void after a level switch, and watching the host stops at walls and starts fresh after a cutscene.
- **Lobbies.** One machine whose map never loads can't hold up the start. A broker that drops us is tried less often. Picking an MCC level asks the host before anything changes. Split screen players who join a game in progress can pick their profile and team (thanks LukeShortCloud).
- **Settings.** config.toml is written whole or not at all, so a crash can't leave half a file.
- **Crashes.** Windows crash reports of a null call show who made the call. The Mac no longer crashes switching from Fullscreen to Borderless. A missing MCC level goes back to the menus with a reason instead of hanging.
- **Platforms.** Every platform's name comes from one list, so the PSP shows by name (thanks Sheepdog01). Logs show `~` instead of your account name. iOS and tvOS build again.

### Security

- Maps are checked more strictly as they load: Custom Edition maps, MCC maps and Stubbs maps that are damaged or made to cause trouble are refused. Pictures are read more carefully too.
- The game is harder to upset from a misbehaving host, and from players and programs that send bad join requests or listing traffic.
- A split screen game no longer opens its network ports to other computers. It's for the players on your machine only, and now only your machine can reach it.
- Steam content, saved profile and game type names, Delta profile answers and update lists are all read more carefully.
- Dedicated servers: the control panel and telnet console are stricter about logins and what they accept, and the deploy scripts install files more safely.

Please update. Hosts, please update your servers too.

### Cross-play and hosts

- **OpenCE 44.** This release plays with OpenCE's build-355 (network version 44), and our players still join their 41 and 43 hosts as before. Our wire is `chupa-44a`.
- **Hosts announce 44.** With this release our hosts announce network version 44, OpenCE's current one. A host announces one number, so players on older Chupathingy builds and OpenCE players still on 41 or 43 can't join a 44 host until they update. Which number our hosts announce follows our signed compatibility table, so it can change without a new release. Servers that set their own number keep it.
- **Map sharing across the line.** Getting a missing Custom Edition map from the host works both ways with OpenCE 42 and later (44 included) and with our earlier test builds.
- **MCC games.** OpenCE players who have the map join our MCC games and play them through. One without the map is told right away that our host doesn't send MCC maps, instead of being left waiting.
- **Dedicated servers** run MCC maps in their rotations (`sv_workshop_get` gets them after a sign-in from the terminal), skip rotation entries whose maps aren't installed, and tell the game list their game type. Servers can share ban lists, lock joins with `sv_lock`, make late joiners wait, and take reports. With `HALO_DEDICATED_CONTROL_HOSTS` set, the control panel answers only to the names listed.
- **`network.public_address`** sets the address a host advertises, for servers behind a relay (thanks Jizzy).
- The server guide covers all of it: server/README.md.

### Consoles

- **Tester builds are out** for the original Xbox, the Xbox 360 and the Wii U (test-20261113), on each Warthog repo's releases page. The Wii U's loading screen colors and the consoles' BUTTON LAYOUT row come in the next ones.
- The original Xbox joins games of up to 32 and hosts up to 16, can run at 60 frames a second if you set it (30 is still the default), has its own title ID and icons, and draws a big match's lights as retail did. An older settings file on the Xbox starts a game alone too (thanks Fantasy).
- The Xbox 360 and Wii U take 32 players, and a lot of byte-order fixes reach them: the level, shadows, mirrors, contrails, camo and scripts (thanks Mayo1970 for the script fix). The 360 writes config.toml whole, like the Xbox and the Switch.
- The Delta Guide, voice chat, screenshots, clips, the MCC Workshop and Stubbs mode aren't on consoles yet.

### Android

- **Vulkan, opt-in.** thelinkin3000's Vulkan renderer is in the Android app. OpenGL ES stays the default; set `display.renderer = "vulkan"` to try it. If Vulkan can't start, the game uses OpenGL ES and asks before changing anything.
